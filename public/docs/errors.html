Errors

Every failure is a SelfholdError. Its code is for your code; its message is written for the person, so show it as it is; its docUrl links here.

try {
  await wallet.writeRecords(request, { account: address });
} catch (error) {
  if (error.code === "Declined" || error.code === "Closed") return; // a cancel
  if (error.code === "NeedsCoins") return askForCoins(error.address, error.needed - error.balance);
  if (error.code === "AccountChanged" || error.code === "NotConnected") return refreshAccount();
  showError(error.message);
  console.warn(error.code, error.docUrl);
}

PopupBlocked

The browser blocked Selfhold's window.

Why
Your page called connect, writeRecords or signMessage after an await, or outside a click, or the person turned pop-ups off for your site.
What the person saw
Nothing. The window didn't open.
What to do
Make the call the first thing your click handler does. Pass work that must happen first as a promise (see Read first). If the person blocks pop-ups, the message asks them to allow them.
Safe to try again
Yes, from a click.

Closed

The person closed the window before it answered.

Why
They closed the window or its tab, or navigated it away.
What the person saw
Their own action.
What to do
Treat it as a cancel. Nothing was signed.
Safe to try again
Yes.

Declined

The person declined.

Why
They pressed Decline, or stopped the fee work.
What the person saw
Their own choice.
What to do
Treat it as a cancel. Nothing was signed. Selfhold for Chrome waits a few seconds after a decline before it takes the same site's next request.
Safe to try again
Yes, if they ask.

Reloaded

The window was reloaded in the middle of a call.

Why
The person reloaded Selfhold's window.
What the person saw
The window starting over.
What to do
Nothing was signed, and the call isn't resent. Let the person try again.
Safe to try again
Yes.

AccountChanged

The wallet uses another account now.

Why
The account you passed isn't the wallet's account any more: the person switched accounts, or signed in with another passkey.
What the person saw
Nothing, for this request.
What to do
Nothing was sent. Read wallet.address() again, redraw your page for that account, and rebuild the write.
Safe to try again
After you rebuild it.

NotConnected

This site has no account from the wallet.

Why
You passed an account, but the wallet isn't connected to your site: the person disconnected it, or it was never connected.
What the person saw
Nothing.
What to do
Ask them to connect again (wallet.connect(), from a click).
Safe to try again
After connecting.

NeedsCoins

The account can't pay for the write.

Why
The account doesn't hold the coins for the write's payments and fee, and no fee sponsor could pay the fee. The error is a NeedsCoins, with address, needed and balance (bigints), and sponsor: why the sponsor didn't pay ("down", "dear", "most"), or null if none was asked.
What the person saw
The window's review, then its message.
What to do
Show the person how many coins they need and where to send them: needed - balance more, to address. If sponsor is "down", trying again in a few minutes may work. See When work can't pay.
Safe to try again
Once the account holds the coins, or the sponsor is back.

NoWallet

Selfhold for Chrome isn't installed.

Why
You made the wallet with use: "extension", and the extension isn't in this browser.
What the person saw
Nothing.
What to do
Offer Selfhold's window instead (use: "window" or the default), or a link to install Selfhold for Chrome.
Safe to try again
After installing.

Lost

Another write landed in this one's place.

Why
Your check returned a string on three reads in a row: the records changed in a way this write can't now apply to.
What the person saw
Their write sent, then your page's message.
What to do
Show the message your check returned. Read the chain again, and build a new write only if the person still wants it.
Safe to try again
As a new write, built from what the chain holds now.

NotRecorded

Sent, and still not recorded after 30 minutes.

Why
The node took the write but no block recorded it: it was most likely dropped.
What the person saw
Their write sent, then slow, then late.
What to do
Read the chain first. If the write isn't there and is still needed, let the person send it again.
Safe to try again
After checking the chain.

Failed

Anything else.

Why
Something the other codes don't cover: a host that couldn't be reached, a write the chain refused, a passkey that couldn't make a key.
What the person saw
The wallet's message.
What to do
Show message: it's written for the person. Report what you can't explain.
Safe to try again
Depends on the message.

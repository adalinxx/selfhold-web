WEBSITE / PRIVACY

A simple page.
No wallet connection.

This website introduces Selfhold. It does not create wallets, connect to your wallet, request recovery phrases, or submit transactions.

What this website stores

The website contains no analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, cookies, forms or browser-storage features. Fonts and images are served with the page; no third-party resources are loaded automatically.

Hosting and external links

A web host receives information needed to serve a page, such as your IP address and browser request. Hosting providers may keep access logs under their own policies. This repository does not configure analytics or a public hosting service.

When you follow a Chrome Web Store or GitHub link, or contact support by email, those services process information under their own policies.

The wallet has its own policy

The extension stores wallet data locally and connects to user-selected nodes. Read the wallet privacy policy for its storage, network and backup details.

support@lattice.build. Never send recovery phrases, private keys, passwords or backups.
